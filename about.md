# Custom Menu Music 🎵

**Custom Menu Music** allows you to replace the default Geometry Dash main menu loop with any custom audio track of your choice (.mp3, .ogg, .wav).

---

## ✨ Features / Características

- 🎧 **Any Audio Format:** Supports `.mp3`, `.ogg`, and `.wav` files.
- 🎨 **100% Texture Pack Compatible:** Automatically matches your installed texture pack's button style (circular, hexagonal, themed, etc.) using `CircleButtonSprite`.
- ⚡ **Real-Time Audio Reloading:** Change songs or toggle settings on the fly—the music updates instantly without needing to restart the game.
- 🌐 **Multi-Language Support (English / Español):** Choose your preferred language for in-game notifications and messages.
- 🔔 **Now Playing Notification:** Optional toast notification showing the title of the song whenever it starts playing.
- 🔁 **Seamless Looping:** Toggle continuous loop playback according to your preference.
- 🔘 **Quick Access Button:** Direct shortcut button located on the main menu's bottom bar to open settings in one click.

---

## 📖 How to Use / Cómo usarlo

### English
1. Click the **Music Note button** on the main menu bottom bar (or go to **Geode** -> **Mods** -> **Custom Menu Music** -> **Settings** ⚙️).
2. Click **Browse** next to **Music File** and select your favorite song.
3. Enjoy your custom music in the menu!

### Español
1. Haz clic en el **botón de la nota musical** en la barra inferior del menú principal (o ve a **Geode** -> **Mods** -> **Custom Menu Music** -> **Ajustes** ⚙️).
2. Haz clic en el botón de examinar junto a **Archivo de música** y selecciona tu canción favorita.
3. ¡Disfruta de tu música personalizada en el menú principal!
